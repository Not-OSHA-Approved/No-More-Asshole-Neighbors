# Scoring model

Version 0.3.0 evaluates each eligible property on two independent 100-point
scales. The scores are never averaged because an excellent property with an
impossible transaction is not currently acquirable.

## Retirement Fit

| Category | Points |
|---|---:|
| Privacy and isolation | 30 |
| Utilities and immediate RV readiness | 15 |
| Legal freedom and property-use restrictions | 15 |
| Usable land, storage, workshop, solar, and fleet space | 12 |
| Long-term affordability | 10 |
| RV access and emergency evacuation | 8 |
| Water access | 5 |
| Internet options | 3 |
| West Coast Florida location | 2 |

Privacy is deliberately dominant. Two wooded acres with no visible neighbors
can outrank ten exposed acres.

## Acquisition Feasibility

| Category | Points |
|---|---:|
| Verified solution to the no-overlap problem | 35 |
| Minimal upfront cash | 25 |
| Owner financing and seller flexibility | 20 |
| Affordable payment, balloon, and early-payoff terms | 15 |
| Title and closing flexibility | 5 |

The preferred transaction allows Chris and Becky to secure and occupy the new
property, move the RV, animals, vehicles, boats, shed, and equipment, sell the
current house vacant, and use the proceeds to pay off the new property.

## Hard vetoes

The engine marks a property ineligible before interpreting either score when it
has any of these verified conditions:

- HOA
- no legal road access
- clearly unaffordable housing cost
- serious title problem
- no legal residential path
- impossible access for the 40-foot, approximately 26,000-pound RV
- blocked emergency evacuation route

Unknown information is not a veto, but it earns few or no points and remains a
verification requirement.

## Important thresholds

- Total monthly housing cost of $900 or less is acceptable; lower is better.
- $901 to $1,200 is tight; more than $1,200 is unaffordable.
- Owner financing, minimal cash upfront, payment deferral, sale contingencies,
  early possession, and coordinated closings may solve the overlap problem.
- A three-to-five-year balloon is acceptable when early payoff is allowed.
- Water within 20 miles is good and within 30 miles is acceptable.
- Internet preference is fiber, cable, 5G, then Starlink.
