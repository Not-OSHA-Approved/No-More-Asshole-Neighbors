Fits the mission

Ugly can be useful.

  • At least two wide, usable, high-and-dry acres
  • Power, water, and septic already present and usable
  • Uninhabitable but legal house for RV camouflage
  • Powered sheds, barns, workshops, and outbuildings
  • Home and Grace hidden from the road by distance or vegetation
  • Room for a solar gate, fleet parking, catdominium, and projects
  • Overgrowth and small trees that Chris can clear or mulch
  • Owner financing, assumable debt, delayed funding, or flexible closing

Does not fit

Expensive problems we cannot fix.

  • HOA, hostile deed restrictions, or prohibited RV occupancy
  • Official condemnation, demolition orders, code liens, or blocked utility reconnection
  • Flood zones, wetlands, soup-like ground, or unreliable evacuation access
  • Skinny flag lots, visible neighbors, or acreage without usable width
  • No legal residential path or no way to hide and occupy Grace
  • Acquisition terms requiring Maltby Road to close before the move
  • Long-term debt that follows Chris and Becky into retirement
